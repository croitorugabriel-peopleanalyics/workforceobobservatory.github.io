Methodology and provenance

The Workforce Health Index is experimental and is not an official statistic. Every metric retains source, indicator code, period and URL. Missing components are excluded rather than imputed.

Company-level data

Comparable company HR indicators are not included automatically because no single standardized public API covers them. Company reporting requires source-specific adapters and a clear comparability policy.
