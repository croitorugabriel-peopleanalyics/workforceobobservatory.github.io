Quality across the journey

Quality across the journey must be treated as an explicit part of the analytical product, not as an undocumented implementation detail. Define the business purpose, population, time basis, owner, control and failure behaviour. Then connect the rule to evidence that can be reviewed when data, structures or stakeholder expectations change. In the context of hr data quality, governance and security by design, this is what makes the outcome reusable, auditable and explainable.
