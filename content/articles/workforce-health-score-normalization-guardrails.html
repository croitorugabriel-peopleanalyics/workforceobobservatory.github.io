This article turns the workforce health score from a headline number into a governed decision aid. The purpose of the score is not to compress complexity for its own sake, but to make overall movement visible without hiding the exceptions leaders must still investigate.

01 · Deep dive

Why normalise indicators

Composite scores mix signals with different units, scales and desirable directions. Normalization is what allows employment rates, engagement proxies or critical-risk indicators to sit in one decision frame without one measure dominating by raw magnitude alone.

02 · Deep dive

Direction before formula

Before choosing a formula, decide what good and bad look like for each indicator. Some measures improve when they increase, others improve when they decrease, and some should not be scored at all without contextual interpretation.

Apply it

Write polarity rules in business language first, then encode the math. That sequence makes validation easier when leaders challenge the outcome.

03 · Deep dive

Bounding the scale

A bounded scale improves comparison, but it also creates design choices about clipping and saturation. If the bounds are too loose, critical movement disappears. If they are too tight, normal operational variation starts looking like a crisis.

04 · Deep dive

Dimensions and weighting

Weighting is a leadership choice disguised as a formula choice. It reflects what the organization considers strategically material, and it should be reviewed when business priorities change.

Apply it

Test optimistic, pessimistic and concentrated-risk scenarios before locking the weighting model so you can see which components dominate the final number.

05 · Deep dive

Why averages hide exceptions

A strong overall score can coexist with one collapsing component if other indicators compensate for it mathematically. That is why the score should always travel with component visibility and exception logic.

06 · Deep dive

Guardrails protect decisions

Guardrails turn the model from a summary statistic into a decision product. They specify when a component override, threshold breach or confidence issue prevents the organization from treating the aggregate score as “healthy”.

07 · Deep dive

How to read the model

Executives need a simple reading path: overall direction, component contribution, exception status and recommended action. The platform should make that order obvious so the score supports judgment instead of replacing it.
