This article expands the LinkedIn presentation into a practical framework for People Analytics leaders, practitioners and data teams. It focuses on the choices that make workforce information trusted, explainable and decision-ready.

01 · Deep dive

Responsibilities before tools

Responsibilities before tools must be treated as an explicit part of the analytical product, not as an undocumented implementation detail. Define the business purpose, population, time basis, owner, control and failure behaviour. Then connect the rule to evidence that can be reviewed when data, structures or stakeholder expectations change. In the context of a reference architecture for an hr datamart, this is what makes the outcome reusable, auditable and explainable.
