This article expands the LinkedIn presentation into a practical framework for People Analytics leaders, practitioners and data teams. It focuses on the choices that make workforce information trusted, explainable and decision-ready.

01 · Deep dive

From fixed reports to open questions

From fixed reports to open questions must be treated as an explicit part of the analytical product, not as an undocumented implementation detail. Define the business purpose, population, time basis, owner, control and failure behaviour. Then connect the rule to evidence that can be reviewed when data, structures or stakeholder expectations change. 02 · Deep dive

Semantic contracts

A business term should resolve to an approved population, reference date, definition, calculation rule and quality expectation. This turns language into governed analytical behaviour instead of leaving an AI system to select a plausible interpretation.

Apply it

Document the rule, assign an owner, define a threshold or acceptance condition, and specify what users see when the control fails.
